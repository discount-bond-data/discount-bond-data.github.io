KR-T yields

Transfer-learning zero-coupon yield curves for Germany, Japan, the United Kingdom, Canada and Switzerland, jointly estimated with U.S. Treasuries.

File format and coverage

All yield files begin with date, min_observed_ttm, max_observed_ttm and max_curve_ttm - _days in daily-maturity files, _months in monthly-maturity files - followed by one column per maturity.

FieldMeaning
dateObservation date (YYYY-MM-DD)
min_observed_ttm_days / _months Shortest maturity of the domestic bonds used in estimation on that date
max_observed_ttm_days / _months Longest maturity of the domestic bonds used in estimation on that date
max_curve_ttm_days / _months Last maturity with a published KR-T value: the endpoint of the curve actually published on that date

On each date, KR-T yields are published only up to the last maturity supported by an observed domestic bond or a synthetic bond priced using FX forwards within the observed FX forward range.

Yields are annualized, continuously compounded, on a 365-day year, in decimal units, and cells past max_curve_ttm are blank. Every variant is published as CSV; the daily-observations, daily-maturities variant is also published as Parquet, with the same columns.

Data availability

A date is published only when domestic bond data, U.S. reference data, FX spot and FX forward quotes are all available, so the KR-T dates for a market can differ from its KR dates. The international government bond quotes and the FX spot and forward rates come from Bloomberg. Date-level inclusion and exclusion reasons are available in the bond coverage detail files.

Construction

Local bond markets have maturity gaps: on many dates no domestic bond sits near the maturity of interest. KR-T fills them from the deepest market available. U.S. Treasuries are converted into the local currency through FX spot and forward rates, and the domestic and converted bonds are fitted jointly, under a penalty on the curvature of the cross-currency spread that leaves its level and slope free. Where the domestic cross-section is already dense, KR-T reproduces standalone KR.
