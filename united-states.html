KR yields

Details and file format →

Kernel Ridge Regression zero-coupon yield curves for U.S. Treasuries. 10-, 20-, and 30-year estimates begin on June 14, 1961, January 5, 1973, and November 25, 1985, respectively.

Bond market condition measures

Details and file format →

Idiosyncratic Treasury Volatility (IT-VOL) measures idiosyncratic volatility normalised by overall volatility. Treasury Market Complexity (T-COM) measures the complexity of the term structure.
